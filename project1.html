En la Nube (Podcast)

Project Description

Everyday, new technological approaches are shifting our way of life. Most of the tech notes and reports that address these shifts everyday do not arrive to the ears of the hispanic community through serious publishers. With this project, I aim to bring a channel for the hispanic community to be up to date with the most crucial technological advancements that are affecting or will affect, directly and indirectly their way of life. En la Nube Podcast, narrated by Denisse Guerra in latin american spanish will be a medium for the hispanic people that has a minimal understanding of the most recent tech innovations to learn, generate own, informed opinions from unbiased information, in the format of 10-15 minutes podcast episodes.

Key Topics

In this Podcast, a wide variety of tech news and topics will be presented and discuss, primarily addressing 3 fields: Cloud Computing, AI and Cybersecurity .
